# Artwork

Both assets were drawn with the built-in GPT Image tool in Codex. The first generation is the transparent nine-pose atlas. The second is the field guide's woodland illustration. The app embeds its artwork in the executable.

## Sprite atlas prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a production-ready 3 by 3 sprite atlas PNG with actual transparent alpha. EXACTLY NINE isolated whole-body poses of PIP, the SAME little raccoon. Nine equally sized invisible square cells, each pose entirely inside its own cell, with generous clear gutters on all sides. No grid, text, labels, scenery, floor, stickers, ground shadows or extra floating marks. Square canvas. Every pose is a beautifully inked and colored vintage naturalist cartoon for a premium whimsical desktop companion, tactile dry-brush ink lines with lovely tapered weight and restrained crosshatching, muted warm gray-brown fur, deep espresso black raccoon eye mask and paws, luminous warm ivory muzzle and inner ears, russet-orange tied neckerchief with a little knot. Exaggerated pear-shaped body, perky ears, shiny expressive simple round eyes, extraordinarily fluffy striped tail. Distinct raccoon, NOT red panda, no glasses or hats. Crisp graphic silhouettes readable at 100 pixels, expressive and appealing. Consistent proportions and scarf. Each pose is a small narrative vignette. All look generally toward the RIGHT with the bushy banded tail sweeping to the LEFT. Big clean face. 
Row 1: (left) jaunty upright bipedal WALK frame, left foot reaches forward to the right, arms in opposition; (middle) next WALK frame: right foot now planted, left foot lifting, tail swings up slightly, same right-facing walk, same camera and scale; (right) startled AIRBORNE dangling with splayed limbs, surprised round eyes and tail aloft.
Row 2: (left) SLEEPING in a compact little curled circle, tail around pointed nose, eyes shut; (middle) sitting and happily munching a round golden butter biscuit held in both paws, puffed cheeks; (right) squinting through a tiny brass spyglass looking far to the right, explorer following a link.
Row 3: (left) a serious little accountant crouching over a miniature wooden bead abacus held in paws, counting; (middle) inspecting a tiny green circuit board with one tiny bright beetle on it through a magnifying glass, bug hunting; (right) staggered with a long folded ivory manuscript unrolling over his paws, comically overwhelmed by reading. Every prop belongs completely inside the animal's cell and touches the body. Fine hand-drawn personality, collectible picture-book quality, no words anywhere.

## Woodland plate prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Single landscape illustration for the habitat plate of a vintage pocket field guide. Wide horizontal aspect 3:2. A beautiful soft sage green woodland clearing in a 1940s naturalist children's book, antique two-colour lithography on warm pale parchment. Composition: framing airy fern fronds, thin trunks and a few mushrooms at the two far outer edges only, softly hatched green-grey conifers and low rolling hills in the far distance, a pale hazy butter-yellow sun in the upper right, beautiful little paper flecks, distant pine silhouettes. The central 55% is a quiet open clearing for a separately added animal sprite. Mossy ground at bottom quarter; open pale cream-mint atmosphere above and behind. A few sharp fine foreground leaves at lower left and lower right, plentiful negative space. Purposefully limited palette of desaturated eucalyptus, olive and warm ivory, painterly subtle ink grain and aged silkscreen textures with exquisite editorial restraint, light and tranquil, quiet afternoon. Depict nature alone. No animals, no people, no typography, no decorative borders, no watermarks, no text, no numbers. Beautiful bespoke illustrated book endpaper, full bleed.
